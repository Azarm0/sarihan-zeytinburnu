# Source notes

This page is an unsolicited concept. The restaurant has not commissioned it,
has not approved it and does not know it exists. It carries a visible banner
saying so.

## Contact details on the page are placeholders

The real telephone number, the exact map coordinates, the door number and the
link to the restaurant's Google Maps listing were removed on 2026-08-01. They
identified and routed people to a real business on a page that business never
agreed to.

The phone number now shown uses the Turkish `555` reserve range, which never
routes to a person.

## Menu and prices

Menu structure and prices were taken from the restaurant's published online
menu and reflect a June 2025 price update. They are illustrative for a design
concept and should not be presented as current.
